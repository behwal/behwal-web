01 · Domino chain puzzle

Line them up. Give one push. Make sure the Zomblock falls dead last.

Every domino is a character with a trick. The Ninja vanishes and drops in wherever you tap, the Pirate blows a hole in the floor, the Builder bridges the gap, the Aviator flies up to the ceiling. Arrange the squad, then a single push decides everything.

  • 5 worlds

02 · Calm farm puzzle

Tap an animal and it walks until something is in its way. Bring every animal home.

A quiet valley, a herd with a mind of its own. Sheep go straight, cows take up two squares, a hen flutters over whoever blocks her, the pig turns round when it meets a wall, the bull flattens fences, and Clover the sheepdog herds the stragglers. Play in the sun for a calm stroll, or in the rain against the clock.

  • 200 fields at launch
  • Sun or rain
  • 0 lives, free hints

03 · Relaxing path puzzle

Tread lightly. Every spot you leave sinks behind you.

Visit every stepping stone and land on the flower last. Each bird moves its own way: the jacana steps, the heron goes any direction, the hummingbird hovers over gaps, the kingfisher darts to the next perch, the urutau swoops in an L under the moon, and the laughing gull glides on the diagonals. Painted marshes, real bird calls, no timers.

  • 6 birds
  • 720 levels
  • 0 timers

Someone else is dreaming. Something else is trying to get in.
